How to play

Solid = hit Striped = avoid
  • Click, tap, or use the keyboard on each solid target as quickly as you can.
  • A normal target starts at 100 points. Each consecutive hit adds a 10-point combo bonus, up to 300 points per hit.
  • Striped decoys cost 75 points and reset your combo.
  • Missing a target costs 0 points but resets your combo. Empty-space clicks and expired real targets count as misses.
  • Every mode gets harder during the round. Easy stays larger/slower; Hard stays smaller/faster.

ReactionBlitz play area

During a round, targets and decoys appear here as buttons. Keyboard players can use Enter or Space. Press Tab to leave the active game control.
